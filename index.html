🦤 dodo_rl — teaching a Dodo to walk (and backflip)

Reinforcement Learning · NVIDIA IsaacLab · 2026

Reinforcement-learning locomotion for an 8-DOF bipedal robot. 256 parallel robots stumble their way to competence in simulation — no hand-crafted gaits, just a reward that says "go forward, don't fall" — with the goal of sim-to-real transfer. Pipeline: URDF → USD → RL env → PPO (rsl_rl) → trained policy → real robot.

PyTorchIsaac SimPPOsim-to-real8-DOF biped
sim_env :: reward = forward_velocity − fall_penalty
live sim · walk → backflip → repeat
